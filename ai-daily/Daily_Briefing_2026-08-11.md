## 1. How America’s Dizzying AI Strategy Plays Into China’s Hands
**Source:** Center for Security and Emerging Technology  
**Link:** [How America’s Dizzying AI Strategy Plays Into China’s Hands](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/how-americas-dizzying-ai-strategy-plays-into-chinas-hands/)  

**So What:** US AI strategy inconsistencies may undermine competitiveness with China

The Trump administration's AI strategy, which promotes AI and semiconductor exports while addressing national security concerns, may inadvertently create inconsistencies that China can exploit to gain a competitive edge. This could lead to a loss of US technological leadership and decreased national security. The US must balance its AI strategy to mitigate these risks and ensure a cohesive approach.

**Watch For:** Future US policy decisions on AI exports and national security to assess their impact on the US-China tech competition

## 3. Putting Armageddon on Autopilot: How Artificial Intelligence Could Make Nuclear Threats More Effective
**Source:** War on the Rocks  
**Link:** [Putting Armageddon on Autopilot: How Artificial Intelligence Could Make Nuclear Threats More Effective](https://warontherocks.com/putting-armageddon-on-autopilot-how-artificial-intelligence-could-make-nuclear-threats-more-effective/)
