**Source:** Center for Security and Emerging Technology

## 5. AI for science needs reasoning, not just data
**Source:** Artificial intelligence – MIT Technology Review  
**Link:** [AI for science needs reasoning, not just data](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/08/10/1141384/ai-agents-for-science/)  

**So What:** AI's potential to accelerate scientific discovery depends on its ability to reason and generalize

While AI has made significant progress in data analysis, its limitations in reasoning and generalization hinder its potential to drive scientific breakthroughs. To fully leverage AI in science, researchers must develop more advanced AI systems capable of complex reasoning and abstraction. This will require interdisciplinary collaboration and investment in AI research.

**Watch For:** Advances in AI reasoning capabilities and their application in scientific research
