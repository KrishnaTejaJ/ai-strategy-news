## 1. Taiwan’s Tech Hurdles Threaten Military Capability and Potential Coalition Defense
**Source:** Center for Security and Emerging Technology  
**Link:** [Taiwan’s Tech Hurdles Threaten Military Capability and Potential Coalition Defense](https://cset.georgetown.edu/article/taiwans-tech-hurdles-threaten-military-capability-and-potential-coalition-defense/)  

**So What:** Taiwan's limited military AI readiness threatens its ability to deter Chinese aggression and collaborate with partners.

The gap between Taiwan's world-leading semiconductor industry and its limited military AI readiness highlights the need for strategic investment in AI adoption for military applications. This discrepancy could have second-order effects on regional security dynamics, as Taiwan's ability to defend itself and collaborate with partners is critical to maintaining a balance of power in the region. Effective AI adoption could enhance Taiwan's military capabilities and bolster its position in the face of Chinese aggression.

**Watch For:** Monitor Taiwan's efforts to address its military AI readiness gap and potential collaborations with partners to enhance its defense capabilities.

## 2. Nvidia’s China Partners and the PLA
**Source:** Center for Security and Emerging Technology
